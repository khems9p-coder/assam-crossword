Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Assam Crossword app ("the app") collects, why, and how you can control it. The app is made by KBX Studios.

1. Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. As a guest, your progress, scores, best times, achievements and settings are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

You can remove this data at any time with Settings > Clear local data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Creating an account

If you create an account, we collect your email address, the display name you choose, and your password. Accounts are handled by Firebase Authentication, a service of Google. Your password goes directly to Firebase; the app never stores or sees it after you type it.

Your game progress (points, level, puzzles completed, best times, streaks and achievements) is saved in Cloud Firestore, a Google database, so it can be restored on another device.

3. The leaderboard

If you have an account, your display name, points, level and number of puzzles completed are shown on the public leaderboard to other players. Please do not use your full real name or anything private as your display name. Guests are not shown on the leaderboard.

4. Feedback

If you send feedback by email or through the app, we receive the message and any name or email address you include. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

5. What we do not collect

The app has no advertising and no advertising trackers. It does not use analytics. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

6. Where your data is stored

Account and progress data are stored by Google Firebase. The Cloud Firestore database for this app is located in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Google's own privacy policy applies to its services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and progress until you delete your account. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app: My Profile > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your saved progress and your leaderboard entry. If you cannot use the app, email us from your account's email address and we will delete it for you.

9. Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. Younger children can play as guests, which does not send any personal information to us. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and publish the new version in the app and on our website.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: kbxchetry@gmail.com (KBX Studios).
